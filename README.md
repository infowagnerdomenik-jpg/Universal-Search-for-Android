## 🚀 What's new in 2.0.0

Version 2.0.0 is a complete rewrite – **100 % native Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive**. Faster start, smoother animations, and a home screen you can arrange exactly the way you want.

---

## 🎛 Customization

Everything is yours to arrange.

- **Two custom tabs** – rename them, pick an icon (90+ Material symbols, any emoji or your own image), turn them on or off
- **Layout editor** – move, add, hide and remove cards like widgets; drag handle or long press; changes apply when you tap *Save*
- **Every card in any tab** – even several times; home and search of each tab are arranged separately
- **Launcher widgets** – add widgets from your installed apps as cards on the home screen, with adjustable height
- **Navigation buttons** – Home, Work and Favorites with own names, icons and on/off switch
- **Quick search bar** – at the top or as a bar above the keyboard
- **Phone and tablet / foldable** – separate layouts, two columns on large screens
- **20 languages**

## 🔍 Search

### On your phone
- **Apps** – typo-tolerant, including app shortcuts
- **Contacts** – by name, email or phone number
- **Calendar** – understands *today*, *tomorrow*, *weekend*, dates and whole sentences like *"dentist Friday"*
- **Files & media** – with thumbnails and file type icons
- **Notes** – search your notes or save a new one right from the search bar
- **Calculator & unit conversion** – *"12 × 7"*, *"5 km to mi"*, temperatures, data sizes …
- **Settings shortcuts** – jump straight to about 40 Android settings pages

### On the web
- **Web cards** – weather (with official severe weather warnings), places, answers, knowledge, dictionary, top stories
- **Results without ads** – from your chosen search engine
- **Places nearby** – from OpenStreetMap, free and without a key
- **Address search** – suggestions with distance, route in one tap
- **Movies & music** – cards with cast, track lists, trailers and where to stream in your country
- **Recognize a song** – via Audile, Shazam or Google

### Your Google account *(optional)*
- Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Tasks and contacts – right in your search results

## 🏠 Home

A home screen that shows what matters – before you type.

- **Today** – date, weather, your next event, the most urgent task, alarm, birthdays and your latest notifications
- **Music** – what's playing with controls, or your favorite music service with its shortcuts
- **Agenda** – your upcoming events
- **Apps & contacts** – suggestions and the ones you use most
- **Recent files** and **notes**
- **Google cards** – upcoming events, open tasks, unread mails, recent documents
- **Pinned cards** – pin any web card (e.g. weather) and keep it up to date
- **Launcher widgets** – any widget of your installed apps as a card

## 🔒 Privacy

- **Free providers only** – one switch turns off Google and other proprietary services
- **Permissions on your terms** – internet and app search can be switched off inside the app; every permission is explained
- **No ads** – result cards are ad-free
- **Keys stay safe** – API keys are encrypted in the Android Keystore and never part of backups
- **Your data stays on your device** – notification history and recently played music are never backed up
