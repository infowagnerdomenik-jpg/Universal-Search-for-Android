Home screen

What matters, before you type

DownloadAll features

Before you type a single letter, Universal Search shows what is relevant right now: today’s events, the weather, your alarm, birthdays, music and notifications – and the apps, contacts and files you are most likely to need.

Today

The “Today” card is a calm overview of your day. Every part can be turned off, and parts without content simply disappear.

Highlight

Your day at a glance

Date and a weather chip that opens the full weather card, the next event with “now running” or “in 45 min” and a route to it, all-day and further events via “+N” – plus your next alarm, birthdays with age and the route to work in the morning or home in the evening.

Notifications

The last two notifications – tap opens, swipe also removes them from the status bar. A 48-hour history grouped by app.

Weather

Refreshes silently in the background; until then the last state is shown.

Alarm & birthdays

Re-read whenever you come back, so the overview is always current.

Music

The music card is always there – also in the online tab.

Highlight

What’s playing

Cover, title, artist and app icon with previous, play/pause and next – the cover fills the card in its own color, with a little wave while it plays.

Nothing playing?

Your preferred music service with its logo, its own shortcuts, suggestions like liked songs or Supermix, and recently played.

Not installed yet

An “Install” button leads straight to the store. With Bluetooth headphones connected: “Continue listening”.

Everything else

Order and visibility of all home sections are yours – arrange them in the layout editor.

Agenda

Upcoming events like in Google Calendar – weekday above the day number, today in a circle, calendar color strip. Swipe away what you don’t need.

App suggestions

Suggested and frequently used apps, with a button to all apps.

Frequent contacts

One tap opens the contact sheet with all ways to reach someone.

Recent files

Recently opened files; the number is up to you.

Notes

Your newest notes directly on the home screen, the rest via “All notes”.

Pinned cards

Cards you pinned from the web search – each can be moved and hidden.

Launcher widgets

Widgets of your installed apps as cards, with adjustable height.

Home · Work · Favorites

Your places one tap away, each with its own name and icon.
