StartSearch

Search

Your phone and the web – in one place

DownloadAll features

One search bar for everything: apps, contacts, events, files, notes and settings on your phone, and answers, knowledge, places, movies and music from the web. Results appear while you type, and the search understands small typos and whole sentences like “appointments tomorrow” or “dentist Friday”.

The search bar

The search bar is built to stay out of your way: multi-line input that reacts instantly, results that arrive together in one calm step, and controls right where your thumb is.

Highlight

Fast, calm, precise

The input is decoupled from the content below, so typing never lags. Results follow after a short pause and appear together – no flickering lists.

Search history

Tap the empty field to see recent searches. Tap inserts a term, swipe sideways deletes it.

Clipboard suggestion

Text you just copied (at most three minutes old) can be searched, opened as a link or dismissed.

Live suggestions

Suggestions while you type; the arrow puts one into the field so you can keep editing.

Keyboard on your terms

Opens automatically always or only at app start. Long-press the keyboard button to switch auto-open off and on.

Special searches

One button opens address search, movies & music, or song recognition – shown filled while active.

Quick search

11 services one tap away – YouTube, YouTube Music, Amazon, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Spotify, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, Maps and Play Store – as a row at the top or a bar above the keyboard.

Details

  • Search bar shows the active search engine’s icon; tap it for a quick engine picker
  • Enter – also on hardware keyboards or with pasted text – starts the search instead of a line break
  • Clear button and a send button that changes shape when pressed
  • Quick search opens the service’s app or the browser; without the app it leads to the Play Store
  • Apps button next to the keyboard button opens the app drawer
  • Tapping a card, button or chip closes the keyboard
  • Haptic feedback on taps, toggles and swipes

Search your phone

Local search runs in the background and never leaves your device. You decide which sections appear and in which order.

Highlight

Calculator & units

Basic arithmetic, brackets, powers, percent and “X % of Y”, functions like sqrt, sin, cos, tan, log and ln – plus conversions like “5 km to mi” for length, weight, volume, temperature, area, speed and data sizes.

Calendar

Title and location of events in the calendars you choose. Understands today, tomorrow, weekend, next week and dates like 24.12.

Settings shortcuts

Jump straight to about 40 Android settings pages – hotspot, VPN, battery saver, Do not disturb, developer options and more. Keywords in English and German, slang included.

Apps & shortcuts

Find and launch installed apps, typo-tolerant, with up to 8 hits. App shortcuts are searchable too; hidden apps stay hidden.

Contacts

Names and emails typo-tolerant, phone numbers from 3 digits. Tap opens a rich contact sheet.

Files & media

Files on the device with thumbnails, colored file-type icons and shortened paths. Install APK files directly.

Notes

Finds text in your own notes. Type “Note …” to save the rest as a new note right from the search bar.

Calculator

  • Notations ×, ÷, ·, comma as decimal sign, colon as divide, implicit multiplication before brackets
  • Units written out in English and German (miles, inch, pound, gallon …)
  • Copy the result or open your calculator app

Calendar

  • Date words: today, tomorrow, yesterday, day after tomorrow, this week, next week, weekend, weekdays
  • Swipe sideways hides an event – with undo

Files

  • Full file access or only images, videos and audio
  • Number of results configurable
  • Open with the matching app

Search the web

Online, the search shows rich cards instead of a list of links – from Google Search or, if you prefer, entirely from free sources like Wikipedia, Wiktionary and Open-Meteo.

Highlight

Weather with official warnings

Temperature, condition and feels-like, an hourly forecast and days as tiles, expandable values for precipitation, wind, humidity and air quality – and official severe weather warnings of the German Weather Service with severity, duration and instructions.

Answers & knowledge

Direct answers with source, knowledge cards with facts from Google or Wikipedia.

Dictionary

For single words: part of speech, meanings, pronunciation and hyphenation from Wiktionary.

Top stories

Current news with source and time.

Results without ads

The first result large, the rest compact – and never an ad. Related questions go straight into the search field.

Pin any card

Pin a card – like the weather – to your home screen. It shows its last state instantly and updates in the background.

Your search engine

Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Ecosia, Brave, Perplexity, Qwant – or up to 20 of your own.

More

  • Results of another search engine, parsed without ads, with a button to its results page
  • Each card’s open button leads to the matching app or the source
  • Numbers and texts roll or crossfade smoothly when they update
  • Optionally share your precise location for weather and places instead of guessing from your connection
  • Open results in a Custom Tab or your browser

Places & addresses

Find places nearby and addresses anywhere – free from OpenStreetMap, without a key and without a limit.

Highlight

21 kinds of places nearby

Pharmacy, supermarket, bakery, fuel, charging station, ATM, restaurant, café, hospital, doctor, toilets, parking, post office, hotel and more – with opening hours like “closes at 8 pm”, phone, website, route and wheelchair access.

Address search

Suggestions like in Google Maps with distance. Tap shows the place, the arrow starts a route, the star saves it as a favorite.

Home, Work & favorites

Several addresses per type with an optional name. One tap starts the route; with several addresses a picker opens.

Your maps app

Google Maps, the device’s maps app (Organic Maps, OsmAnd, CoMaps …) or OpenStreetMap in the browser. Google Maps links are rewritten for your choice.

Details

  • Source: OpenStreetMap (Photon) or the device’s place search
  • Search automatically after a short pause or on button press
  • Opening hours evaluated: open 24 hours, “closes at …”, “opens at …”
  • Home, Work and favorites with their own name and icon, each can be turned off – the row never leaves gaps
  • License note “© OpenStreetMap contributors” with link

Movies & music

Look up movies, series, artists, albums and songs – with cast, track lists, trailers and where to stream them in your country.

Highlight

Rich cards that open in place

Poster or cover, introduction from Wikipedia, facts like release, director, cast, genre, seasons or label, album track lists from MusicBrainz – and buttons for the trailer, your streaming apps, JustWatch and IMDb.

Your own media

Music and videos on your phone with title, artist, album, length and cover – played in your default player.

Online catalog

Movies, series and music from Wikipedia and Wikidata, songs from MusicBrainz – even with typos and unfinished words.

Recognize a song

One button starts Audile, Shazam or Google song search.

35 countries

Streaming and music providers of your country, chosen automatically via SIM or language – or set by hand.

Streaming providers

  • Worldwide: Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV, HBO Max, Paramount+, Crunchyroll, MUBI, Pluto TV
  • Germany: Joyn, RTL+, WOW, MagentaTV, waipu.tv, ARD, ZDF, arte, 3sat
  • Austria: ORF ON · Switzerland: Play SRF, RTS, RSI
  • United Kingdom: BBC iPlayer, ITVX, Channel 4, NOW · USA: Hulu, Peacock, Tubi
  • Plus France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, India, Brazil, and Viki in Korea, Japan and Taiwan

Music services

  • Worldwide: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Tidal, Deezer, Amazon Music, Qobuz, SoundCloud, YouTube
  • Regional: JioSaavn, Gaana, Anghami, Boomplay, Audiomack, Yandex Music, VK Music, Melon, LINE MUSIC, KKBOX, QQ Music
  • Songs open straight at the track when its ID is known
