This website uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Fonts, images and logos are served from this website itself – there are no connections to Google Fonts or other third parties when you visit it.

When you open the website, GitHub processes technical access data (in particular your IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and operating system) in server log files. This is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its security and stability.

Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable presentation of this website. Data may be transferred to the USA; the transfer is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU standard contractual clauses. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Saved theme and language

If you change the color mode or the language of the legal pages, this choice is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is required.

Links to other services

This website links to GitHub (source code and downloads) and Telegram (community group). No data is sent to these services until you click such a link. After that, their own privacy policies apply, e.g. GitHub and Telegram.

Your rights
