Personalize

Tabs, layout, design and widgets – your way

DownloadAll features

Universal Search adapts to you: two tabs you name and fill yourself, a layout editor for every surface, Material 3 Expressive design with your colors, a home screen widget and deep Android integration.

Two custom tabs

Highlight

Each tab, its own world

Start with “Local” and “Online” – or build something completely different. Every tab has its own home screen and its own search sections; any element can go into any tab, even several times.

Name & icon

A free name and a free icon: over 90 Material symbols with search, any emoji, or your own image (SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, HEIF …).

Per tab

Keep the home screen below the results while typing, and choose which special searches the tab offers.

Switch your way

Use the buttons below the search bar or swipe left and right anywhere – sideways lists take priority.

Smooth transitions

Switching tabs is a full-screen crossfade: what stays the same does not move.

Layout editor

A full-screen editor for four surfaces: home and search of each tab – shown exactly in the shape of your screen, with sample data.

Highlight

Move cards like widgets

Long-press and drag to move, tap for an element’s options, add any element to any surface – even several times. Changes apply when you tap Save. The top area with tabs, navigation buttons and quick search stays fixed.

Launcher widgets

Add widgets of your installed apps as cards and adjust their height.

Hide & restore

Hidden elements are kept aside and can be shown again any time.

Tablets & foldables

Full width and two columns on tablets; foldables are arranged separately for outside and inside.

Permission hints

An element that lacks a permission says so – tap to grant it.

Design

Highlight

Material 3 Expressive

Own shapes and color roles per area, stacked cards and shape animations. Choose Material You, one of nine color themes – blue, green, red, orange, yellow, turquoise, purple, pink – or any color you like.

Light, dark & AMOLED

Automatic or fixed, with an extra dark mode in true black.

Wallpaper

Your wallpaper behind the cards – brighten or darken it separately for light and dark mode.

Blur

Blur behind cards, menus and wallpaper, per group with its own intensity.

Details

Rounded outline icons only, original logos of services, weather icons in the app’s style and colored file-type icons.

Widget & tiles

Highlight

Search bar widget

Tap opens the search instantly, without a splash screen. Choose the buttons – Google Lens, voice search, Gemini, music – and an optional round button for music or Maps.

Your widget design

System colors, nine color themes, Nothing OS style or a custom color; brightness, AMOLED, corner radius and transparency – with live preview.

Quick Settings tiles

Two “Search” tiles open the search right from the notification shade.

Always current

Reacts to night mode and new Material You colors and redraws after a reboot.

Android integration

Digital assistant

Set Universal Search as your assistant app – also via the voice command key.

Instant start

No splash from widget, tiles and assistant; from the app icon only until the home screen is ready.

Tablets & foldables

Own layouts with columns; on foldables phone view outside, tablet view inside.

20 languages

Follows your system or set per app; AI-translated languages are labeled.

Themed icon

The app icon follows your wallpaper colors, with a monochrome version for themed icons.

Update check

Checks GitHub releases and shows the status in the settings.
